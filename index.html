Quick Guide

Scroll to browse your photos. Tap any photo to open the film viewer — swipe left or right, or tap a frame on the film strip to jump around.

Tap the ★ star on any photo you love. A "My Favourites" button appears at the top so you can review just your picks.

Save all your starred photos at once using the bar that appears at the bottom. No need to download one by one.

This gallery is available for a limited time. Make sure to save your favourites before the window ends.
